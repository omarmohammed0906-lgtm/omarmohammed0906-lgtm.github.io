Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This site and the Omar's Shorts uploader are operated by Omar Mohammed for his own channels only. The uploader is a private tool that connects to the owner's own YouTube and TikTok accounts through the platforms' official APIs (Google OAuth and TikTok Login Kit) solely to upload videos and read basic statistics for those videos.

Data we access: an access token for the owner's account, basic account information needed to post (such as the channel or creator name), and view counts of our own videos.

Data we store: access tokens and video history are stored only on the owner's computer. We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties, and we do not collect data about viewers or other users.

Google API Services: our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data deletion: access can be revoked at any time in Google Account permissions or TikTok settings; stored tokens are then deleted.
